Common Questions
Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know about booking with Precious Care.
How are sitters vetted?
Every sitter goes through a comprehensive background check, is CPR/AED/First Aid certified, and is personally interviewed and vetted by our founder Adeline.
What ages do you cover?
We care for children of all ages, from newborns through teenagers. Our Blessed Beginnings program specializes in newborn care.
What areas do you serve?
We serve families throughout Southeast Michigan including Troy, Rochester Hills, Sterling Heights, Birmingham, Bloomfield Hills, and surrounding areas.
How much does it cost?
Hourly pricing is based on the sitter age, experience, client expectations, and number of children/pets. Contact us for a personalized quote.
Do you offer pet sitting too?
Yes! We offer dog walking, cat care, feeding, playtime, and overnight pet sitting from sitters who love animals.
