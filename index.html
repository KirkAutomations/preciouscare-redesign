Our Story

Founded by Adeline Jones

Founded in 2020, Precious Care has grown from a focused operation into a full-service childcare partner. Located in Southeast Michigan, Michigan, we serve automotive, industrial, and commercial clients who demand quality without compromise.

Our commitment to customer satisfaction drives everything — from the first CAD drawing to the final inspection. Whether it’s a single prototype or a production run, every part gets the same attention to detail.
