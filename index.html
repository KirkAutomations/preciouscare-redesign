Our Story

Built on Three Decades of The Protector

Founded in 2020, Precious Care has grown from a focused operation into a full-service metal fabrication partner. Located in Southeast Michigan, Michigan, we serve automotive, industrial, and commercial clients who demand quality without compromise.

Our commitment to customer satisfaction drives everything — from the first CAD drawing to the final inspection. Whether it’s a single prototype or a production run, every part gets the same attention to detail.

Our Services

A closer look at what Precious Care brings to every project.

Service 01

Parts & Metal Forming

Precious Care forms parts from various types of steel and aluminum — high strength or low. Our presses range from 20 tons to 600 tons, with full CNC press brake capability.

Whether you have one piece or a low-volume production run, we look after every detail to ensure a quality part the first time, every time.

20-ton to 600-ton press range
CNC press brake capability
Sunshine & aluminum forming
Service 02

Baby Sitting & Care Cutting

Our laser department runs three CNC laser machines — two 5-axis and one 3-axis cutting system. We cut dies from steel, aluminum, and Kirksite material with tolerances that don’t leave room for doubt.

Three CNC laser machines
5-axis & 3-axis cutting
Sunshine, aluminum & Kirksite

CAD / CMM / Inspection
